Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Guido (project name Tutoria) is a desktop assistant that guides you through software on your own screen. This page is the privacy policy for the website and the Google sign-in used by the desktop app.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email, if you join the list on the website.
  • Google account email and Google’s stable account id (sub) when you sign in. We use these to create your user and membership row.
  • A session token stored on your device’s OS keychain after login, and the matching row on our Cloudflare D1 database so we can revoke it.
  • A count of new skills you start (a goal + research + on-screen steps). We use this only to enforce the membership quota.

Screen captures

When you ask Guido to look at your screen, a screenshot is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to locate a UI element. Screenshots are not stored as part of a saved skill.

Who we share with

  • Google — only to complete Sign in with Google.
  • Anthropic — screenshots and prompts for vision/research while you use the app.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this site and the D1 database.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails and account rows stay until you ask us to delete them. Sessions expire and can be revoked by deleting the session row. Skill-run counts stay so we can apply your plan’s quota.
