Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Guido (project name Tutoria) is a desktop assistant that guides you through software on your own screen. This page is the privacy policy for the website and the desktop app. Guido is currently run by an individual developer, not an incorporated company; "we" below means that developer.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email, if you join the list on the website.
  • Your email address and a hashed password when you create an account. We never see or store your password in plain text.
  • A session token stored on your device's OS keychain after login, and the matching row on our Cloudflare D1 database so we can revoke it.
  • A count of new skills you start (a goal + research + on-screen steps). We use this only to enforce the membership quota.
  • The number of seconds of audio you transcribe through voice input each month. We use this only to enforce the voice quota — see "Voice input" below for what happens to the audio itself.
  • If you subscribe to a paid tier, your billing history and subscription status. Payments are processed by Stripe — we don't receive or store your full card number.

Screen captures

When you ask Guido to look at your screen, a screenshot is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to locate a UI element or understand the application you're in. Screenshots are not stored as part of a saved skill and are not retained by us after the request completes.

Voice input

When you use voice input, a short audio clip (up to 120 seconds) is sent from the desktop app, through our server, to Aqua Voice for transcription. We do not store the audio ourselves — only the transcribed text is returned to the app, and only the clip's duration is logged, to enforce the monthly voice quota. We can't make retention promises on Aqua Voice's behalf; if you want details on how long they hold audio they receive, ask us and we'll point you to their policy.

Who we share with

  • Anthropic — screenshots and prompts for vision/research while you use the app.
  • Aqua Voice — audio clips, for speech-to-text transcription, if you use voice input.
  • Stripe — payment details and billing history, if you subscribe to a paid tier.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this site, our server, and our database.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails and account rows stay until you ask us to delete them. Sessions expire and can be revoked by deleting the session row. Skill-run and voice-usage counts stay so we can apply your plan's quota, and reset each billing period.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the data we hold about you, or to export it, by emailing us at the address below. We'll respond within a reasonable time. Deleting your account removes your user, session, and quota rows; it does not retract data already sent to Anthropic or Aqua Voice for a request you made before deletion, since we don't control their retention.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or share changes, we'll update this page and move the "Last updated" date above. We don't currently email users about changes, so check back if you want to stay current.
